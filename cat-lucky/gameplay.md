# 🎮 Gameplay

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/cat-lucky.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Earn greater rewards with each new floor! Beware of lurking monsters, always ready to steal your winnings. You can collect rewards at any time and exit the game whenever you choose. Each play costs 10 tickets and earns points. Plus, you recover 1 free ticket every hour, with a maximum of 30 tickets available. Dive into the game and uncover exciting surprises!
