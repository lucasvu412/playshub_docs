# ⚡ Cat Promotion

In the _Cat Promotion_ feature, you have the chance to elevate your Cat Hero’s power by boosting its level and increasing base stats. Each promotion level adds a 25% boost to your Cat's core power, allowing it to reach new heights in strength and resilience. With every enhancement, your Cat Hero becomes better equipped to face tougher challenges and defend against alien invasions.

**How to Promote Your Cat Hero:**

1. **Choose Materials**: Select the materials required to advance your Cat’s level.
2. **Invest PLAYS Coins**: Spend a designated amount of PLAYS coins to complete the promotion process.
3. **Boost Cat Power**: Each level promotion increases your Cat’s power stats by 25%, enhancing its combat abilities.

⚠️ Note: Success depends on the materials selected. If unsuccessful, the materials will be used, and promotion won’t be achieved.
