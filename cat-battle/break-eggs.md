# 🥚 Break Eggs

Players can gather over 1,000 Shards to unlock the chance to mint exclusive items or Cats, each with unique attributes. When breaking eggs, each minted item or Cat follows specific rarity rates, offering players the opportunity to uncover rare and valuable characters. Higher rarity Cats are especially sought after, as they come equipped with enhanced superpowers that add greater strength and value to your collection.

* **Gather Shards**: Collect Shards throughout your journey to reach the 1,000 Shard threshold needed to break eggs.
* **Mint Rare Characters**: Eggs can mint rare and high-power Cats, increasing your combat capabilities.
* **Enhanced Superpowers**: Higher rarity Cats grant players superior skills and attributes, making them invaluable allies in battles.

Take advantage of this exciting feature and enhance your roster with powerful new Cats by breaking eggs today!&#x20;
