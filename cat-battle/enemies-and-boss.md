# 👽 Enemies & Boss

In **Cat Battle**, the galaxy is under siege by a powerful alien menace, and it’s up to you and your feline warriors to defend it! Each stage introduces new alien enemies with unique abilities, strengths, and weaknesses.

#### Enemies

The invading forces from planet Zogara come in a variety of forms, with each enemy class requiring different tactics to defeat. Some enemies are fast and agile, while others are heavily armored, making them harder to defeat without strategic upgrades. Each enemy poses a unique challenge, keeping battles fresh and demanding constant adaptation as you progress.

#### Bosses

At the end of each stage, prepare to face off against the **Boss Alien**. These formidable foes are far stronger than standard enemies, boasting unique powers that require quick reflexes and a well-upgraded Cat Hero to overcome. Bosses escalate in difficulty, pushing your strategic skills and Cat Power to the limit. Defeating a Boss rewards you with valuable Shards and other resources, aiding your journey to become the ultimate guardian of the galaxy.
