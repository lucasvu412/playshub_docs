# 🤑 P2E, P2A, W2E

PLAYS Hub brings a unique gaming experience where every action counts towards exciting rewards. In our **Play-to-Earn** model, players can engage in games to earn valuable in-game assets, such as Shards, Gems, or even cryptocurrency like TON. Take part in challenges, climb the leaderboards, and claim your rewards as you progress.

With **Play-to-Airdrop**, active players have the chance to participate in exclusive airdrop events, gaining access to rare items, special tokens, and unique collectibles just for being part of the community.

Additionally, with our **Watch Ads to Earn** feature, players can watch short ads to earn resources like free tickets, Gems, or Shards, helping to fuel your gameplay journey without any extra cost.

Dive into the PLAYS Hub ecosystem and turn your gaming skills into real-world value!
