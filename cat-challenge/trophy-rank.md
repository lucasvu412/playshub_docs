# 🏆 Trophy Rank

After each victory, players earn 2-3 trophies, while each defeat deducts 2-3 trophies. The top 1,000 players each week will be rewarded, and at the end of the week, the leaderboard resets to kick off a new competition. Get ready to battle, earn trophies, and claim your spot among the best!
