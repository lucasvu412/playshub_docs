# 🥇 PvP League

Each day, players receive 3 tickets to enter the arena and climb the leaderboard by battling other players and earning trophies. Complete daily tasks to earn additional tickets and maximize your chances to advance. Every season lasts two weeks, with the top 1,000 players at the season’s end claiming major rewards in TON. Join the competition, win trophies, and aim for the top in this thrilling PvP showdown!
